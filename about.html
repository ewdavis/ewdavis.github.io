About

Symbiotic Arc is a cognitive science and machine intelligence research laboratory based in Washington, DC. We study the architecture of minds, biological and artificial, and build systems grounded in that science. Our work spans perception, affect, geometric representation, and human-machine interaction and translates theory into systems that solve real problems.

We specialize in alternative foundational methods that enable sovereign, local, machine intelligence that provide trustworthy outcomes for their users. Your data is priceless, and once given away to a big company, can never be private again. Our architectures enable a new future with that privacy is paramount.

Founder

Eric W. Davis, Ph.D., is a scientist and engineer specializing in machine intelligence, autonomous systems, and human-machine teaming. His research focuses on cognitive architectures that draw from neuroscience, dynamical systems theory, and geometric methods.

Eric has spent his career building systems at the intersection of biological and artificial intelligence. His work has spanned theory of mind, autonomous decision-making, sensor fusion, and the mathematical foundations of representation learning.

Approach

We believe the path to trustworthy machine intelligence runs through the science of how minds actually work. Current approaches optimize statistical correlation yielding performance on narrow benchmarks. The problems that matter require systems that model causal structure, adapt to context, and produce outputs that can be formally verified.

Our research draws on multiple traditions: computational neuroscience, topological data analysis, operator theory, and geometric cognition.

Consulting

We partner with organizations navigating hard problems in AI architecture, autonomy, human-machine teaming, and evaluation methodology. Engagements range from technical advisory and architecture review to embedded research partnerships.

If you are working on a problem where the standard approach has hit a wall, we would be excited to help you find solutions.
